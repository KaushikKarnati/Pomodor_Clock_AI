Effective October 15, 2026. This policy describes FocusTalkAi, published by Ram Karnati.

App data

FocusTalkAi stores your tasks, plans, session history, and settings locally on your device. The app does not send this information to the developer and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not require an account.

Voice and AI

Microphone access is used when you choose voice input. Speech recognition is configured to run on device, without a cloud transcription fallback. The app does not save microphone recordings as audio files. Task planning uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models when available; the app does not send your tasks to an external AI service. Apple manages system services and any required model downloads under its own privacy policies.

Notifications and Live Activities

Local notifications and Live Activities can display task or session details on your Lock Screen. You can control their visibility and permissions in iPhone Settings.

Storage and deletion

You can remove session history entries in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data; device backups and restores are controlled by your Apple settings. The developer does not hold a remote copy of your task history.

Support and beta testing

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and attachments you send and use them to answer your request and investigate issues. Do not include sensitive information unless needed for your request. If you participate through TestFlight, Apple may provide crash reports, usage information, and feedback under its TestFlight terms and privacy policy.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics or tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service under its privacy statement.
