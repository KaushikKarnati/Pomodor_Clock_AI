FocusTalkAi brings a tactile tomato timer and on-device task planning to your iPhone.

Need a hand?

Email kaushik.karnati@icloud.com for help, feedback, or a bug report. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive task content.

Set a timer

Swipe the tomato dial to set a duration, then tap Start. Pause and resume whenever you need. Sounds and haptics can be adjusted in the app's settings.

Plan with your voice or keyboard

Speak or type your tasks and the time you have available. Voice input needs microphone and speech recognition permission. Apple Intelligence planning requires a compatible device and supported settings. Manual planning and the timer remain available without AI.

Notifications and the Lock Screen

Allow notifications in iPhone Settings to receive completion alerts. Focus modes, notification preferences, and sound settings can affect alerts. Live Activities show the current session on the Lock Screen when enabled.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 26 or later. English is supported. Apple Intelligence features depend on device, language, and system availability. No account is required.
